Account and data controls

Delete a Nova Ledger account

Deleting a Nova Ledger account permanently removes access to the account and requests deletion of its associated asset records.

Request deletion inside the app

  1. Sign in to Nova Ledger.
  2. Open the account menu in the upper-right corner.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation message and confirm the request.
  5. Nova Ledger records the request and signs the account out.

Request deletion from the web

Email briankendallsoftware@gmail.com from the email address used for the Nova Ledger account. Use the subject Nova Ledger account deletion and state that the account and associated asset records should be deleted.

Never include the account password or an authentication code. Brian Kendall Software may ask the requester to verify control of the account email before processing the request.

Email deletion request

Data covered

The request covers the authentication account, user identifier, user-entered assets, and associated deletion-request records. After verification, a valid request will be completed within 30 calendar days.

Information that must be retained temporarily for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or a legal obligation will be removed or de-identified when that need ends. See the privacy policy for full details.
