Official support

Your wealth, clearly.

Nova Ledger is a focused personal asset tracker. Add assets manually, see one calculated total, update values, hide balances on screen, and keep every signed-in portfolio separate.

Email support

What it does

Nova Ledger stores user-entered asset names and values, calculates a portfolio total, and lets users edit or delete their records.

What it avoids

It does not connect to a financial institution, move money, execute trades, sell investments, or provide financial advice.
