Privacy policy

Brian Kendall Software provides Nova Ledger, a manual personal asset-tracking application. This policy explains what the app collects, why it is used, how it is protected, and how deletion works.

Information collected

Nova Ledger collects the email address used to create and operate an account. Supabase assigns an authenticated user identifier so the service can keep each account separate. Users enter asset names and values, and the app stores labels and display percentages with those records. This information is treated as personal financial information.

Nova Ledger does not request contacts, camera, microphone, photos, precise location, health information, payment credentials, bank credentials, or an advertising identifier. It does not use advertising or cross-app tracking technology.

How information is used

The email address and user identifier support account creation, authentication, account management, support, and record ownership. User-entered asset information provides portfolio tracking, calculations, editing, display, and deletion. Nova Ledger does not use these records to execute transactions, make investment decisions, or provide financial advice.

Service provider and sharing

Nova Ledger uses Supabase for authentication, database storage, and related cloud infrastructure. Information is sent to Supabase only to operate the application. Brian Kendall Software does not sell personal information or share it with advertising networks or data brokers.

Information may be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a lawful business transfer. Any service provider must process information only for the services it supplies.

Security

Nova Ledger sends application data over encrypted HTTPS connections. Supabase authentication identifies the signed-in user, and PostgreSQL row-level security restricts portfolio and deletion-request records to their authenticated owner. No system can guarantee perfect security, so users should protect their email account and password and never send credentials to support.

Retention and deletion

Account and asset records are retained while the account is active so Nova Ledger can provide the requested service. A user can delete individual assets inside the app at any time and can request permanent account deletion through the app or the public deletion instructions.

After verifying the requester, Brian Kendall Software will complete a valid account-deletion request within 30 calendar days. The current production hosting plan does not provide project backups. If the hosting plan or backup configuration changes, this policy and the deletion procedure will be reviewed before the change is relied upon in production.

Records may still be retained temporarily when required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or a legal obligation, and then removed or de-identified when that need ends.

Children

Nova Ledger is intended for adults and is not directed to children. The store listing targets users who are 18 or older. Brian Kendall Software does not knowingly collect information from a child through Nova Ledger.

Changes and contact

This policy may change when the application, service providers, legal requirements, or data practices change. This page will show the effective date, and material changes will be communicated through an appropriate product or store notice when required.
